## Renderer references

The terminal-rendering approach was informed by
[`sinclairzx81/zero`](https://github.com/sinclairzx81/zero), which is licensed
under MIT, specifically its half-block truecolor blitter and JavaScript
object-as-shader material pattern. Arcade's implementation is original; no
source was copied verbatim.

The shape-matched glyph mode in `src/engine/glyph.ts` follows the technique
described in Alex Harri's article
["ASCII rendering"](https://alexharri.com/blog/ascii-rendering): per-character
coverage-shape vectors matched by nearest neighbor. Arcade's implementation is
original.
